The atlas in this directory is extracted and cleaned from the author’s animated GIF preview. Twenty ship designs were isolated, their backgrounds made transparent, and eight animation frames retained per ship. Four ships that were static in the original preview were given small additional lighting/bobbing variations. Image size: 512 × 1280 pixels; each sprite cell: 64 × 64 pixels.
