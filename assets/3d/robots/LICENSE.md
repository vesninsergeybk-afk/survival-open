# Quaternius animated robots (CC0)

Source author: Quaternius, https://quaternius.com/
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These three original GLB models are mirrored from:
https://github.com/danvanderboom/Aetherium/tree/main/samples/unity/Aphelion/Assets/ThirdParty/Quaternius

- custodian.glb (engineer/maintenance robot; idle, walk, run, punch, hit, death)
- overseer.glb (foundry mech; idle, walk, run, shoot, hit, death)
- sentinel.glb (heavy armed guard; idle, walk, run, shoot, attack, death)

Mission 00 always retains its separate Kenney CC0 2D sprite fallback.
Animated 3D is capped at two figures on desktop and one on mobile, and
automatically disabled when render quality turns off enemy 3D models.
No gameplay, hitboxes, AI or wave sizes depend on the optional render layer.
